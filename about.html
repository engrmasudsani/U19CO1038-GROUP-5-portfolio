01 About

About Me

I'm Masud Sani, a final year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, based in Kaduna, Nigeria. My interest sits at the boundary of hardware, software, and artificial intelligence, I like systems where a model's prediction eventually triggers something in the physical world, whether that's a camera flagging an object or a machine being fixed and running smoothly again.

Alongside my degree, I've spent time as an intern at Mahir Computers, where I diagnose and resolve hardware and software issues, train users on best practices, and keep systems running efficiently. That hands on troubleshooting background feeds directly into how I approach AI and embedded projects: test early, measure the real impact, and don't trust a design until it's been under load.

I'm currently building depth in Python, PyTorch, and computer vision, and exploring embedded vision on Arduino/ESP32-CAM hardware, the space where my AI interests and my hardware background meet.

02 Values

How I approach engineering work

Rigor

Measure, don't assume

A design that only exists on paper hasn't earned my trust yet. I'd rather prototype early and see what real data says.

Clarity

Document as I build

Future me is going to open this code with zero memory of today, so I try to leave it readable.

Practicality

Fix what's in front of you

Years of hands-on computer maintenance taught me that the real problem and the interesting problem aren't always the same one, and the real one comes first.
